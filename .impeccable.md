## Design Context

### Users
Shoppers looking for premium audio gear across earphones, speakers, and headphones. They need to browse by category quickly, compare products with confidence, and reach a detail page that explains why a product is worth buying.

### Brand Personality
Refined, modern, and confident. The interface should feel premium without becoming cold, with strong product storytelling and clear purchase paths.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a clean editorial ecommerce direction with warm neutrals, dark anchors, generous spacing, sharp typography, and product imagery as the hero. Avoid generic dashboard styling, heavy glassmorphism, and noisy gradient effects.

### Design Principles
1. Make product discovery effortless through strong category entry points and fast search.
2. Present products like premium objects with spacious layouts, contrast, and focused hierarchy.
3. Keep purchase flows clear and predictable with minimal visual friction.
4. Use motion and interaction sparingly to reinforce quality, not decoration.
5. Maintain consistency across homepage, listing pages, and product detail pages so the catalog feels like one system.
